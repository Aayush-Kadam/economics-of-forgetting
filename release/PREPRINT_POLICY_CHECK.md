## Exact relevant policy

Springer Nature defines a preprint as the author’s manuscript before formal peer review. Its current support page states that preprints may be posted at any time during peer review and that posting is not prior publication and does not jeopardize consideration by Springer Nature journals. Its updated policy explanation states that authors may use any lawful preprint-sharing platform and may choose the preprint license. ETB’s journal-specific instructions contain no contrary preprint prohibition. Springer Nature also supports research-data and reproducibility sharing.

The post-acceptance accepted manuscript and the publisher’s version of record are different objects and may be governed by embargo and sharing restrictions. This release must therefore remain clearly labeled as a pre-review working paper and must not later be relabeled as an accepted manuscript without a fresh policy check.

## Restrictions

- State prominently that the manuscript is a working paper and has not been peer reviewed.
- Do not claim submission, acceptance, publication, or ETB affiliation.
- Do not upload a future accepted manuscript or version of record under these preprint conclusions without rechecking the applicable license/embargo.
- Preserve accurate versioning and link later versions transparently.
- The current repository license is all rights reserved; public visibility does not grant a general reuse license.
