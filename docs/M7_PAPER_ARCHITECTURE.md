# M7 paper architecture

## Recommended anchor

A theory-first paper on why a coarse adverse lifecycle state can support different record persistence than a clean state when institutions cannot condition fully on type or current quality.

## Proposed sequence

1. Minimal two-state model and strict-contingency theorem.
2. Economic decomposition: deterrence, stale-score loss, and rehabilitation response.
3. Counterexamples showing zero value and tiny value.
4. Finite-horizon computational illustration, explicitly regional.
5. Direct punishment and punishment-flag equivalence as interpretation tests.
6. Empirical or institutional discussion of constraints on direct penalties.

## Claims to drop

Universal optimal half-life; earned forgetting; exact prediction/welfare alignment; a general memory-conflict index; exhaustive robustness.

## Claims to retain cautiously

A simple lifecycle-contingent rule can beat the best constant rule in meaningful model regions; the gain survives several functional forms, horizons, limited heterogeneity, and recurrence stresses; curvature and instrument constraints govern magnitude.
