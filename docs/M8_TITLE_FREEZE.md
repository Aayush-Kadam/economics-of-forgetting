# M8 title freeze

Final title: **Persistent Records and Rehabilitation Incentives**.

The title names the two objects actually modeled and does not imply that the paper solves a universal social forgetting problem. “The Economics of Forgetting” was rejected as the final title because the design section is appendix-only and no universal optimal duration survives. “Endogenous Future Outcomes” was rejected because it overstates the incremental novelty relative to endogenous-quality reputation models.
