# M7 functional-form robustness

The two-state gain is positive under logistic opportunity slopes 3, 6, and 10, linear opportunity, and a hard threshold. Magnitudes range from 0.000270 under linear access to 0.693299 under the hard threshold. Curvature therefore changes economic importance dramatically even though it does not reverse the sign in this battery.

Quadratic effort cost was varied through kappa 0.6, 1.2, and 2.5; gains remain positive (0.0248–0.1818). Other cost shapes and nonlinear state laws were preregistered as unsupported and were not approximated by parameter relabeling. The result is functionally regional, not universal.
