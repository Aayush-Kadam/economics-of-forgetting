## Matrix interpretation

The four ingredients with the strongest prior art are: (i) memory/record limits, (ii) reputation-dependent real investment, (iii) deterrence from records, and (iv) welfare analysis of forgetting. The M0 search did **not** locate one verified paper that makes the exact joint object—continuous memory persistence chosen by an institution, a pre-record misconduct margin, a post-record rehabilitation investment that changes a persistent underlying state, and a decomposition of observed predictiveness into informational and institution-induced behavioral persistence—the central model. This is a differentiation claim, not proof of novelty.
