# M7 repeat misconduct

Twelve stress cases cross private-benefit levels 0.75, 1.5, and 3; detection 0.25 and 0.65; and horizons 12 and 20. All retain positive two-state gains, but only six exceed 0.01. Gains range from 0.00292 to 0.14220. M6.5 also found positive fixed-policy longer-horizon gains in all seven original recurrence points.

The policy does not eliminate recurrence. High expected repeat counts occur in weak-detection/high-benefit cases, so the result is a welfare comparison under gaming, not a claim of behavioral control.
