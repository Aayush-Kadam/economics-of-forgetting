## Bottom line

No credible novelty case rests on fading memory, forgiveness, bounded records, reputation-dependent investment, or state-contingent punishment by themselves. The defensible paper is a narrow theory note whose central theorem combines pre-event discipline and post-event productive rehabilitation under one institutional persistence parameter. The zero-rho decomposition is a supporting conceptual result. Regional feedback organizes the mechanism but should not be sold as an independent first result.

Verified sources include Board and Meyer-ter-Vehn (2013), Bohren (2024), Bhaskar and Thomas (2019), Jovanovic (2021), Pei (forthcoming), and the public Hamilton-Cui project record. Full-text access for the current Hamilton-Cui manuscript and Gjerde-Slotnick remains unavailable, so novelty confidence is capped at MODERATE.
