# M8 journal fit

## Primary — Review of Economic Design

- **Scope:** design and analysis of economic institutions and legal-economic instruments.
- **Fit:** the score-persistence instrument and lifecycle tension fit directly.
- **Theoretical depth:** plausible for a focused regional theorem plus transparent computation.
- **Closest papers:** bounded-memory enforcement and information-design work.
- **Strength:** institutional design question is explicit.
- **Weakness:** the design result itself is appendix-only and reduced form.
- **Likely concern:** insufficient equilibrium foundation and modest novelty.

## Backup 1 — Economic Theory Bulletin

- **Scope:** concise theoretical contributions.
- **Fit:** strongest if submitted as a short theory note centered on two propositions.
- **Theoretical depth:** appropriate after further tightening of assumptions and proof presentation.
- **Strength:** clean separation of score and state persistence.
- **Weakness:** results assemble familiar channels and may be viewed as too immediate.
- **Likely concern:** whether lifecycle asymmetry is more than a transparent juxtaposition.

## Backup 2 — Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization

- **Scope:** theoretical, computational, and institutional work on economic behavior and organization.
- **Fit:** accepts mixed mathematical and simulation methods and institutional applications.
- **Theoretical depth:** adequate, though the paper would benefit from a richer behavioral/institutional application.
- **Strength:** the incentive response to records is central.
- **Weakness:** no empirical component or calibrated application.
- **Likely concern:** external relevance of the normalized computations.

## Stretch / poor current fit

- **Games and Economic Behavior:** too ambitious at present; the paper does not solve a strategic equilibrium and its theorem set is narrow.
- **Theoretical Economics / RAND Journal of Economics:** unlikely without a Bayesian or equilibrium extension and a sharper novelty claim.
- **ACM Transactions on Economics and Computation:** limited algorithmic contribution.

This assessment is about fit, not prestige. No submission is authorized.
